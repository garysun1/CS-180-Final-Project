Cross the Line

A tripod camera sees the same background in every frame. We restyle that background once with an image model, put the real people back, and anyone who crosses the line walks into another world.

Play the portal kiosk · drag the line, switch worlds and set off the glitch on recorded scenes, live in your browser.

Beyond a straight line

The same compositing on three clips, with a different rule for which pixels change.

Footage: OpenCV's public tripod clip (plaza) and Small Crowd and Traffic Video Footages on Hugging Face, MIT licence (street, highway). Stand-ins for our own Sather Gate shoot.

How it works

  1. 1

    Film from a tripod

    The camera never moves, so every frame shares one background.

  2. 2

    Find the empty scene

    The per-pixel median over the clip removes everyone who walks through.

  3. 3

    Restyle it once

    Stable Diffusion 1.5 redraws the empty scene; depth and edge ControlNets keep the buildings in place. {{sd_sec_lo}}–{{sd_sec_hi}} s on a CPU.

  4. 4

    Cut out the people

    Anything that differs from the empty scene is a person. Each is recoloured to the new world's light.

  5. 5

    Composite

    The real side stays untouched; on the other side, people and their shadows go onto the generated world.

output = (1 − w)·F + w·[ a·P + (1 − a)·R·S ]   F filmed · R generated world · a, P person · S shadows · w side of the line

Or split by distance

Instead of a line, distance decides: near stays real, far becomes the other world. Depth comes from one run of Depth Anything V2 on the empty scene, and each person takes the depth of the ground under their feet, so they change gradually as they walk away.

Limits and next steps

  • Stable Diffusion 1.5 is the largest model that runs on a CPU. It sometimes invents changes (it repaved part of the night lawn and removed the traffic cones); underwater, anime and overgrown barely worked, and Van Gogh only at high strength.
  • The footage is public clips standing in for our Sather Gate shoot. Person masks come from a CPU background difference, which gets messy on busy night streets.
  • Next: film Sather Gate, then swap in FLUX.2 Klein or Qwen-Image-Edit on a GPU. The pipeline takes any generated image unchanged.
