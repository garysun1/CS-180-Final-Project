Drag the line across the picture and watch people walk into another world. This runs the kiosk's own code on recorded clips; the live-camera version runs on a laptop with python -m crossover.portal_server. Method and trailer: Cross the Line.

How to play

Each scene is a fixed camera. The page knows the empty scene, so anyone who differs from it is a person. People are cut out every frame and shown in the other world wherever the rule says so.
