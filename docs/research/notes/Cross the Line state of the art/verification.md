# Verification of the key findings

> Research sweep of 27 November 2026. Evidence marks: **read** (the page was opened), **snippet** (search-result text only), **memory** (unverified). Social and product sites were blocked in the session, so product facts are mostly snippets.

Two checkers re-examined the 14 closest neighbours and the 14 most useful things to build on.

## Closest neighbours

I checked 14 claims. Only arxiv.org pages could be fetched. Every company or product host I tried failed to resolve in this session: academy.runwayml.com, higgsfield.ai, contra.com, grail.cs.washington.edu and docs.opencv.org. Product claims therefore rest on search snippets, and some come from two independent snippets.

The two papers, LociArt and Vera, are confirmed from the fetched full text, with one caveat: LociArt shows multi-region results only qualitatively. Runway 'World jumping', Luma Modify Video, Aleph 2.0's date and 30 s cap, Meta Restyle's 10 s cap, Decart XR's measured lag and flat window, and the CapCut split-reality tutorial are confirmed from snippets.

Several details look like researcher embellishment and were not found:
- Teams theme names and the 'removes you, fills the hole, img2img' mechanism.
- Tom's Guide's resolution, frame-rate and background-people observations.
- Higgsfield's 'reuse one locked drive across many worlds'.
- The Aleph Adobe post and the $15 for 50 s pricing claim.
- Aleph Inpainting being retired on 30 Jul 2026.
- Luma's 2026 'Video To Video' rename.

One important correction concerns Higgsfield. Its contact-shadow advice is in the snake, sauropod and recap lessons, not the walking lesson. Its wing-walker lesson says that on a locked-off or steady shot the model struggles to make meaningful change, so the effect is painted in by hand. That is direct evidence that our tripod niche is one the video models handle poorly.

Nothing I found combines our pieces: a fixed camera, a world drawn once, exact person pixels, painted or spatial regions that people cross, and live browser compositing. What is still ours is the combination plus the crossing and real-time behaviour.

New, closer academic neighbours are listed under "closer":
- EditCtrl (Feb 2026): real-time, mask-local, multi-region video editing.
- PAI-Studio (May 2026): uses clean plates as background references for background replacement.
- ANYPORTAL (ICCV 2025): zero-shot background replacement that preserves the foreground and relights it.
- Cinematic Compositing (2606.20233): compositing that harmonises the foreground with a generated environment.
These should be cited. The likely reviewer question is 'why not EditCtrl or ANYPORTAL with a mask?'. The honest answer is that they regenerate per frame on a GPU, while we draw each world once, run on a CPU and in a browser, and decide region membership per person.

**Closer or better, found while checking:**

- EditCtrl: Disentangled Local and Global Control for Real-Time Generative Video Editing (Litman et al., Meta + CMU; arXiv 2602.15031, Feb 2026, rev. Apr 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15031 (fetched). Its compute is confined to masked tokens, it claims about 10x efficiency, multi-region text-prompted edits and 'real-time' in the title (no latency figures in the abstract). It is the closest learned version of our PAINT tool. Still ours: worlds drawn once, CPU/browser, per-person crossing. Project page yehonathanlitman.github.io/edit_ctrl.
- PAI-Studio: Cinematic Video Background Replacement with Camera-Aware Motion (Gao, ..., Mike Z. Shou; arXiv 2606.01399, 31 May 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01399 (abstract fetched; clean-plate detail from search snippet). A DiT that is conditioned on the foreground video plus multi-frame background references; its Cine-Restore subset uses clean plates extracted and inpainted from the source video. It relights the foreground, so foreground pixels are probably not kept exactly. A 30K-clip dataset. It is close to our plate-plus-people layering, with a moving camera.
- ANYPORTAL: Zero-Shot Consistent Video Background Replacement (Gao, Lan, Shuai Yang; ICCV 2025; arXiv 2509.07472). https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07472 (fetched). Training-free; combines a video diffusion temporal prior with image-diffusion relighting; a 'Refinement Projection Algorithm' preserves foreground pixels; runs on consumer GPUs. Project page gaowenshuo.github.io/AnyPortal. It is the best candidate to build on or compare against for 'relight people to each world', our known weak point.
- Cinematic Compositing Using Character-Environment-Harmonized Video Generation Models (arXiv 2606.20233, Jun 2026). https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20233 (snippet only). Joint compositing with foreground lighting harmonisation; its baseline removes the foreground to get a clean background and then relights. Relevant to tinting versus relighting people.
- Higgsfield Academy 'The handheld showcase: wing-walker' lesson (2026). https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/ai-vfx-real-footage/the-handheld-showcase-wing-walker (snippet). It states that on a locked-off or steady shot the video model struggles to make meaningful change, so the effect is painted in. This is direct commercial evidence for the gap our tripod, draw-once pipeline fills, and it is quotable in the pitch.
- Not closer, but checked: I found no 2026 installation or repo with a walk-across-a-line world change in my searches (Diffusion TV arXiv 2609.05404 is knob-driven; 'Visions of Destruction' uses Stable Diffusion landscapes driven by camera shadows). ID-V2V (arXiv 2607.22830, identity-preserving video restylization) appeared in a search, but I did not check its method.

## Things to build on

I checked 13 candidates (the two FXjutsu entries are the same repo) using raw GitHub READMEs and LICENSE files, the Hugging Face API and the npm registry. The GitHub REST API, comfy.org, runcomfy and meta.com could not be reached from this session. All 13 exist. Five claims needed corrections:
(1) FLUX.3 Klein's INT8 build ('int8-convrot') is only in the separate -comfy repo. The diffusers repo has bf16, fp8r and nvfp4. The model is 5.55B parameters and the FLUX Kommunity licence is gated (auto-approve).
(2) ffmpeg.wasm 0.12.15 was released 2025-01-07, not April 2025.
(3) Depth Anything 3 is only partly Apache-2.0. DA3-SMALL, DA3-BASE, DA3METRIC-LARGE and DA3MONO-LARGE are Apache-2.0, but DA3-LARGE, GIANT and NESTED are CC BY-NC 4.0. DA3-Streaming (2025-12-11) runs long videos in under 12 GB of GPU memory.
(4) The Inspire Pack is GPL-3.0, which matters only if its code is copied into the repo.
(5) FXjutsu now names LTX-2.5 (22B), a 24 GB GPU for local runs, and an MIT app licence over model weights under the LTX-2.x Community License.
The most useful things to build on in 12 days:
- BackgroundMattingV2 (MIT, ONNX weights). It is the only matting model here that takes our clean plate as an input.
- SlimSAM-77 and the Xenova segment-anything-webgpu Space (Apache-2.0, still RUNNING). A ready click-to-segment pattern for the brush.
- Mediabunny v1.66.5, released 2026-11-26 (MPL-2.0). mp4-muxer is officially deprecated in its favour.
- For better worlds on a free T4: the FLUX.3 Klein int8 build, or FLUX.2-klein-4B (Apache-2.0) as the low-risk fallback.
- SAM 3.1 (released 2026-03-27, gated, Python 3.12, GPU) for a one-off Colab pass of text-prompted masks. A browser port of the SAM3 tracker exists (onnx-community/sam3-tracker-ONNX).
FXjutsu's README confirms that Toon World uses our order: Clean-Plate first, then restyle the empty plate, then put the real people back on top. It also says why: restyling the empty plate stops a cartoon copy of the person peeking out around them. Still only ours: one image per world drawn on a CPU, the line, ring and painted regions where a person crosses as a whole, live webcam compositing in the browser, and shadows plus colour tinting of people.
